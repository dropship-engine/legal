Dropship Engine Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 October 2026

Dropship Engine is a private seller-automation application used to manage marketplace listings, orders, fulfillment, tracking, reconciliation, and related operational records.

Data we process

The application may process marketplace identifiers and operational data such as listing IDs, order IDs, line-item IDs, fulfillment status, tracking information, fees, refunds, and financial reconciliation data. Buyer information supplied by a marketplace is used only as needed to fulfill and support the applicable order.

Data minimization

The application is designed to minimize retained personal data and does not intentionally retain full buyer shipping addresses after fulfillment when they are no longer required for operation, reconciliation, legal, fraud-prevention, or marketplace obligations.

How data is used

Data is used only to operate the seller account, fulfill paid orders, update marketplace listings and tracking, monitor returns or disputes, reconcile fees and profitability, and meet marketplace compliance obligations.

Sharing and sale of data

Personal data is not sold to advertisers or data brokers. Data is shared only with marketplace, payment, shipping, and fulfillment services when necessary to perform the requested commerce workflow or comply with legal and platform requirements.

Deletion requests

The application subscribes to eBay Marketplace Account Deletion notifications. When eBay notifies the application that an account-deletion request applies, the application acknowledges the notification and is designed not to retain unnecessary buyer personal data.

Security and retention

Credentials and tokens are kept outside public source code and are treated as secrets. Operational records are retained only as needed for commerce operations, reconciliation, fraud prevention, audit, and applicable marketplace or legal obligations.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be directed through the contact information registered for Dropship Engine in the eBay Developers Program.
